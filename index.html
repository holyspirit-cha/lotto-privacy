Lotto Analyzer (로또 분석 추천기) Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Package: com.songdo.lotto · Developer: SangDoo Cha (individual)

1. What the app does

The app analyses the past Korean Lotto 6/45 winning numbers bundled with it, entirely on the device, and shows number combinations with the statistical reasons behind them. When you tap "최신 회차 받기" (get latest draws), or when 7 days have passed since the last draw, it downloads new winning numbers from the public Donghang Lottery (dhlottery.co.kr) lookup address.

3. What we do not collect

The developer operates no server and receives no information about you. No account is required, and no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools are used. No permission other than internet access (such as location, contacts, camera or storage) is requested.

4. Retention and deletion

The only data stored on the device is the downloaded winning numbers, which are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Since the developer holds no data, no deletion request process is needed, but questions can be sent to the contact below.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
